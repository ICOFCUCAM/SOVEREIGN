# Evaluation Log — First 5 Institutions

The objective of this phase is **learning, not revenue**. Recruit **5**
evaluation institutions — not 50, not 500 — and watch where they struggle. These
notes will be worth more than months of speculative development.

## Target profiles (pick 5)
A small, varied set surfaces more than five of the same kind:

| # | Profile | Why it teaches us something different |
|---|---------|----------------------------------------|
| 1 | University department | Senate/board records of permanent value; academic governance |
| 2 | NGO | Lean team, high accountability pressure, donor scrutiny |
| 3 | School network | Multi-site, modest IT, repeatable record types |
| 4 | Local authority | Statutory records, retention schedules, public-record duty |
| 5 | Small ministry / government agency | Data-localisation mandates, multi-step authority chains |

## The success metric
For each institution, the question is binary and unaided:

> Did a real operator complete **Create → Govern → Publish → Preserve**
> without engineering assistance?

If not, the exact point where they stopped is the most valuable output of the whole phase.

## Cross-check against the numbers
The Executive Overview (`/admin/executive`, operator-only) shows the same funnel
in aggregate — homepage → trust/procurement → account → first record → published
→ preserved → upgrade, plus friction (abandoned signups, abandoned creation,
paywall reached, governance refusals). Read the qualitative log **and** the funnel
together: the log tells you *why*, the funnel tells you *how often*.

## Discipline for this phase
Let real evaluators reveal what is actually missing. Do **not** pre-emptively add:
AI features · more dashboards · more analytics pages · more deployment modes ·
more document types · more governance complexity. Capture the objection; resist
the reflex to build before the evidence asks for it.
