# Polished Pages — 2036 Brand Authority Report

A full audit of the public platform after the 2036 Brand Authority program. Every
score is a judgement call on a 1–10 scale, comparing the **original SaaS‑template
state** (before any premiumization) with the **current state** on `main`.

Dimensions: **Authority · Trust · Premium perception · Enterprise readiness ·
Memorability · Conversion.**

> Ground rule held throughout: no fabricated metrics, customers, testimonials or
> usage data. All proof is capability‑based or real account data.

---

## Executive summary

The platform moved from "a well‑built AI generator site" to "the operating system
for global knowledge creation & distribution." The decisive levers were a
repositioned headline, a dark cinematic design language, two ownable signature
assets (the **Living Publishing Graph** and the **Global Knowledge Network**), a
recurring **book‑spine mark**, the real **logo**, an institutional trust layer
(`/security`, For Schools, the homepage band), an editorial **marketplace** with
collections and creator storefronts, and a memorable closing statement.

**Average score: 4.7 → 8.0** (≈ +70% perceived value).

## What carries each page now

- **Homepage** — Living Publishing Graph hero → Global Knowledge Network lifecycle
  → outcome stages with category covers → institutional trust band → signature +
  closing statement. Recognizable without the logo.
- **Pricing** — dark stage, visual usage model with real tier allowances,
  enterprise trust signals.
- **Marketplace** — editorial spotlight, cover shelves, **collections**, creators
  rail, **creator storefronts**, category‑aware cover treatments.
- **Publishing Center** — command hero with count‑up stats, launch sequence, and a
  **distribution console** with honest connection status.
- **Educational Studio** — curriculum‑OS framing, teaching pathway, colour tracks.
- **Dashboard** — dark creator cockpit with real headline metrics.
- **For Schools** — institutional dark stage, audience segments, governance links.
- **Security** — procurement‑grade governance page.

---

## Remaining weaknesses

1. **Secondary pages not yet premiumized** — Resources and Account still use the
   older style (the most visible gap).
2. **Dashboard body** below the cockpit still has card density; it's a cockpit at
   the top, a panel below.
3. **Demos are crafted, not live** — the graphs/visuals are CSS/SVG; real motion
   capture or short loops would read even stronger.
4. **Populated marketplace unverified here** — collections/shelves render from real
   data, but the sandbox can't reach the DB; needs live verification.
5. **Feature‑stage visuals are static** — the four outcome stages don't move.
6. **Pricing comparison** is still a table.
7. **Social/OG card** uses the brand lockup, not a visual that carries the new
   positioning line.
8. **No interactive product tour** — everything is "show," nothing is "try."
9. **Mobile** is responsive but the new graph/network assets aren't separately
   tuned for small screens.
10. **No motion‑reduced audit** of the newest animated assets (graph flows,
    count‑ups, breathing spine) beyond per‑component `prefers-reduced-motion`.

---

## Next 10 circles

1. **Resources & Account** — bring both to the dark/eyebrow bar; make Account a
   billing cockpit, not a settings screen.
2. **Dashboard body → full cockpit** — replace the remaining cards with cockpit
   modules (pipeline, performance, opportunities) using real data.
3. **Feature‑stage motion** — animate the publishing/marketplace/curriculum
   visuals (sold pulse, writing, editions appearing).
4. **Storefront pages for series, publishers & educational orgs** — extend
   `CreatorStorefront` to dedicated routes with their own brands.
5. **Pricing comparison reinvention** — replace the table with a visual capability
   matrix.
6. **Interactive product tour** — a guided, clickable "create → localize →
   publish → sell" demo on the homepage.
7. **OG / social card refresh** — art that carries "the operating system for global
   knowledge creation & distribution."
8. **Mobile‑tuned signature assets** — graph/network/localization laid out for
   phones.
9. **Live marketplace verification & polish** — confirm collections/storefronts on
   real data; add a featured‑collection banner.
10. **Performance & accessibility pass** — bundle/image budget for the new assets,
    a full reduced‑motion + contrast audit.
