## 4 Products and services

Northwind Analytics offers three products: Northwind Dispense, Northwind Insights and Implementation and training.

- Northwind Dispense: Dispensing, stock and electronic-prescription workflow for community pharmacies. Used for: Dispensing, stock ordering, controlled-drug registers. Pricing: Per-site subscription, billed annually in advance. Differentiation (as described by the Company): Built for independents and small chains rather than adapted from hospital systems; set up in a day per site. Roadmap: Prescription-collection reminders for patients (2027).
- Northwind Insights: Analytics on dispensing volumes, margin and stock turns, across one or many sites. Used for: Margin management, buying decisions, multi-site reporting. Pricing: Add-on subscription per site. Differentiation (as described by the Company): Benchmarks a pharmacy against anonymised peers in the Northwind network.
- Implementation and training: Data migration, set-up and staff training. Pricing: Fixed fee per site.

## 13 Growth opportunities and strategic rationale

The Company identifies the following opportunities. They are management's view, not forecasts.

- Ireland beyond Dublin: Fewer than a tenth of Irish community pharmacies are customers; the Dublin team can cover the rest of the country.
- Analytics for wholesalers: Anonymised demand data is already shared with two wholesalers; a paid product for others is in design.
- Pharmacy-services booking: A booking module for vaccinations and consultations would sell into the existing base.

## 14 Material risks, dependencies and diligence considerations

The risks and dependencies the Company has identified, with its mitigations, are listed first; the remaining points come from rule-based checks on its figures, not from a review of its documents.

- Reimbursement changes: Changes to how the NHS pays pharmacies affect customers' margins and their willingness to spend. Mitigation: Contracts run for 36 months; Insights helps customers manage margin.
- Wholesaler integrations: Two wholesaler integrations support ordering for most customers. Mitigation: Both are covered by multi-year agreements.
- Key people: The founders hold much of the product and customer knowledge. Mitigation: A second engineering lead was appointed in 2025; both founders intend to stay through a transition.
