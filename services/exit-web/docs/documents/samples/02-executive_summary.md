## 1 The opportunity

Northwind Analytics is a software-as-a-service business in vertical software, headquartered in the United Kingdom and founded in 2016. It has $21.8M of recurring revenue growing 36% a year. Its shareholders are inviting a small group of qualified strategic and financial acquirers to consider a purchase through a structured, confidential process.

## 2 Financial profile

Northwind Analytics reports $24.6M in trailing twelve-month revenue, with $21.8M of annual recurring revenue growing 36% year over year. It is EBITDA-positive at a 14% margin, with a Rule of 40 score of 50. The ten largest customers account for 22% of revenue.

## 3 Investment highlights

The points an acquirer is likely to weigh most, each drawn from the figures above or attributed to the Company:

- Recurring revenue of $21.8M ARR, up 36% on the prior year
- 78% gross margin; EBITDA-positive at a 14% margin
- Rule of 40: 50 (ARR growth 36% plus EBITDA margin 14%), above the 40 that buyers of recurring-revenue businesses commonly use as a threshold
- Net revenue retention of 117%: existing customers, taken together, spend more each year than the last
- A diversified customer base: the ten largest customers are 22% of revenue
- Network effects reported by the Company: the product becomes more useful as more customers use it
- A proprietary data asset reported by the Company, accumulated through use of the product
