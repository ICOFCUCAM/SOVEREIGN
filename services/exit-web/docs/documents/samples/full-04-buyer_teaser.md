## 1 The opportunity

Project Meridian is a software-as-a-service business in vertical software. It has built a recurring-revenue business growing 36% year over year, and its shareholders are inviting a small group of qualified acquirers to consider a purchase through a structured, confidential process. The Company's identity and detailed financials are disclosed once a mutual NDA is signed.

## 2 Selected metrics

Headline operating profile (absolute figures disclosed under NDA):

- Revenue growth: 36% year over year, recurring-revenue led
- Gross margin: 78%
- EBITDA margin: 14%
- Net revenue retention: 117%
- Rule of 40: 50
- Market: growing 7% a year (company estimate)

## 3 Why this asset

Project Meridian combines recurring revenue with 78% gross margins. The Company reports a proprietary data asset built up through use. Existing customers spend more each year, so growth does not rely on new customers alone.

- 78% gross margin; EBITDA-positive at a 14% margin
- Founders active and available through a transition

## 4 Process & next steps

Interested parties should request the mutual NDA. Once it is signed they receive the confidential information memorandum and staged data-room access, and are offered a management presentation in weeks 2–3 of the process. Indications of interest are due in week 4 (planning assumption).
