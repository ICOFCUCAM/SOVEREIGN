## 1 The opportunity

Project Meridian is a software-as-a-service business in vertical software. It has built a recurring-revenue business growing 36% year over year, and its shareholders are inviting a small group of qualified acquirers to consider a purchase through a structured, confidential process. The Company's identity, detailed financials and the indicative valuation are disclosed once a mutual NDA is signed.

## 3 Why this asset

Project Meridian combines recurring revenue with 78% gross margins. The Company reports network effects and a proprietary data asset built up through use. Existing customers spend more each year, so growth does not rely on new customers alone.

- 78% gross margin; EBITDA-positive at a 14% margin
