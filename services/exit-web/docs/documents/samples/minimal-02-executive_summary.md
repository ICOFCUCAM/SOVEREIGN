## 1 The opportunity

Calder Freight Software is a business in logistics and freight, headquartered in Germany and founded in 2014. It has $8.9M of recurring revenue growing 19% a year. Its shareholders are inviting a small group of qualified strategic and financial acquirers to consider a purchase through a structured, confidential process.

## 2 Financial profile

Calder Freight Software reports $11.3M in trailing twelve-month revenue, with $8.9M of annual recurring revenue growing 19% year over year. It is loss-making at EBITDA level (-4% margin), with a Rule of 40 score of 15. The ten largest customers account for 41% of revenue — a concentration buyers will test in diligence.

## 3 Investment highlights

The points an acquirer is likely to weigh most, each drawn from the figures above or attributed to the Company:

- Recurring revenue of $8.9M ARR, up 19% on the prior year
- 61% gross margin
- Net revenue retention of 102%: existing customers, taken together, spend more each year than the last

## 4 Market position

Calder Freight Software has not yet supplied its market size, growth or competitive position. Buyers will expect an estimate of the addressable market with its source, and a view of the main competitors and how Calder Freight Software wins against them. It serves 210 customers.

## 5 Key considerations

Matters buyers are likely to examine closely, raised now so they can be prepared for:

- The business is loss-making at EBITDA level; buyers will model the path to profitability
- The Rule of 40 score is 15 (ARR growth 19% plus EBITDA margin -4%), below the 40 that buyers of recurring-revenue businesses commonly use as a threshold
- Information not yet supplied (market size, headcount, leadership team, capitalisation table); buyers will ask for it early

## 6 Process & timeline

Outreach is to qualified counterparties on a strictly confidential basis. Interested parties sign a mutual NDA, receive the confidential information memorandum and staged data-room access, and attend a management presentation in weeks 2–3 of the process. Indications of interest are due in week 4, with a signed letter of intent targeted in weeks 5–6. These dates are a planning assumption.
