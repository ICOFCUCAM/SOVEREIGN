## 1 Executive Summary

**Management recommends a competitive sale process for Calder Freight Software. ExitOS estimates the enterprise value to a strategic buyer at $21M (range $12M–$37M), with 13 qualified buyers in view. On the process plan, a definitive agreement would be signed in weeks 8–10 after launch. The Board is asked to approve the proposed resolutions.**

## 2 Background

Calder Freight Software has $9M of annual recurring revenue, growing 19% year over year, and $11M of revenue over the last twelve months, at a 61% gross margin and a -4% EBITDA margin. Net revenue retention is 102%. The ten largest customers account for 41% of revenue. Management believes a sale process now would test the value of the business with the buyers most likely to pay for it. The valuation below is an estimate for setting expectations, not a price any buyer has offered.

## 3 Investment Highlights

1. Immediate market entry — acquiring an operating logistics and freight business rather than building one in-house
2. Customer acquisition — the company's logos and contracts transfer with the transaction
3. Geographic reach — an operating base in Germany
4. Cost synergies — for an acquirer with overlapping operations, shared overheads, platform and go-to-market spend

## 5 Process and Timing

- Week 1 · Launch — Target list confirmed; anonymised teaser sent to qualified buyers
- Weeks 1–2 · NDAs and CIM — Confidentiality agreements signed; the memorandum and staged data-room access released to signed parties
- Weeks 2–3 · Management presentations — Engaged buyers meet the management team
- Week 4 · Indications of interest — Non-binding proposals due from engaged buyers
- Weeks 5–6 · Letter of intent — Preferred party chosen; terms and exclusivity agreed in writing
- Weeks 6–9 · Confirmatory diligence — The preferred party completes its detailed review under exclusivity
- Weeks 8–10 · Definitive agreement — Sale and purchase terms negotiated and signed
- Weeks 10–16 · Closing — Conditions satisfied, funds flow and closing; timing depends on consents and regulatory approvals

## 6 Proposed Resolutions

> **[RECOMMENDATION]** That the Board authorise management to prepare and launch a competitive sale process for Calder Freight Software, with a strategic-buyer enterprise value of $21M (range $12M–$37M) as the reference point for evaluating offers.

1. Authorise management to prepare the sale materials (teaser, confidential information memorandum and data room) and to approach qualified buyers under signed confidentiality agreements.
2. Delegate the day-to-day conduct of the process to a transaction committee of the Board, which reports at each milestone.
3. Require that no letter of intent or exclusivity be signed, and no definitive agreement be entered into, without a further resolution of the Board.
4. Authorise the engagement of legal counsel and, if the committee decides, a financial adviser, on terms the committee approves.

## 7 Basis of Preparation

Figures are as supplied by management, unaudited, in US dollars. The valuation is prepared under ExitOS Valuation Framework v1.0.0; buyer probabilities and the strategic premium are model estimates from comparable transactions. The Company has not yet supplied market size, market growth, intensity of competition, regulatory exposure, competitive position, defensibility, technical architecture, headcount, founders' role, leadership team, capitalisation table, gross retention, customer growth, fully diluted share count; the valuation model used neutral values for these, so the range will move when they are supplied. The timeline is a planning assumption. The proposed resolutions are a starting point for the Company's counsel, not a form of minutes.
