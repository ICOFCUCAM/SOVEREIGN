## 1 Executive Summary

**Management recommends a competitive sale process for Northwind Analytics. ExitOS estimates the enterprise value to a strategic buyer at $125M (range $77M–$221M), with 23 qualified buyers in view. On the process plan, a definitive agreement would be signed in weeks 8–10 after launch. The Board is asked to approve the proposed resolutions.**

## 2 Background

Northwind Analytics has $22M of annual recurring revenue, growing 36% year over year, and $25M of revenue over the last twelve months, at a 78% gross margin and a 14% EBITDA margin. Net revenue retention is 117%. The ten largest customers account for 22% of revenue. Management believes a sale process now would test the value of the business with the buyers most likely to pay for it. The valuation below is an estimate for setting expectations, not a price any buyer has offered.

## 3 Investment Highlights

1. Immediate market entry — acquiring an operating vertical software business rather than building one in-house
2. Technology and data — the proprietary dataset, and the advantage it gives, transfers with the deal
3. Customer acquisition — a durable, expanding base (NRR 117%) to cross-sell into the acquirer's install base
4. Geographic reach — an operating base in the United Kingdom
5. Cost synergies — for an acquirer with overlapping operations, shared overheads, platform and go-to-market spend

## 5 Process and Timing

- Week 1 · Launch — Target list confirmed; anonymised teaser sent to qualified buyers
- Weeks 1–2 · NDAs and CIM — Confidentiality agreements signed; the memorandum and staged data-room access released to signed parties
- Weeks 2–3 · Management presentations — Engaged buyers meet the management team
- Week 4 · Indications of interest — Non-binding proposals due from engaged buyers
- Weeks 5–6 · Letter of intent — Preferred party chosen; terms and exclusivity agreed in writing
- Weeks 6–9 · Confirmatory diligence — The preferred party completes its detailed review under exclusivity
- Weeks 8–10 · Definitive agreement — Sale and purchase terms negotiated and signed
- Weeks 10–16 · Closing — Conditions satisfied, funds flow and closing; timing depends on consents and regulatory approvals

## 6 Proposed Resolutions

> **[RECOMMENDATION]** That the Board authorise management to prepare and launch a competitive sale process for Northwind Analytics, with a strategic-buyer enterprise value of $125M (range $77M–$221M) as the reference point for evaluating offers.

1. Authorise management to prepare the sale materials (teaser, confidential information memorandum and data room) and to approach qualified buyers under signed confidentiality agreements.
2. Delegate the day-to-day conduct of the process to a transaction committee of the Board, which reports at each milestone.
3. Require that no letter of intent or exclusivity be signed, and no definitive agreement be entered into, without a further resolution of the Board.
4. Authorise the engagement of legal counsel and, if the committee decides, a financial adviser, on terms the committee approves.

## 7 Basis of Preparation

Figures are as supplied by management, unaudited, in US dollars. The valuation is prepared under ExitOS Valuation Framework v1.0.0; buyer probabilities and the strategic premium are model estimates from comparable transactions. The Company has not yet supplied intensity of competition, regulatory exposure, competitive position, defensibility, technical architecture, leadership team, customer growth; the valuation model used neutral values for these, so the range will move when they are supplied. The timeline is a planning assumption. The proposed resolutions are a starting point for the Company's counsel, not a form of minutes.
