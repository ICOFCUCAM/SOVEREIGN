# Homepage portraits — "Who Uses Sovereign Dispatch"

Drop a photograph here for each featured role and it appears on the homepage
automatically (the card falls back to a dignified editorial placeholder until
then — never a broken image).

- **Format:** `.webp`, portrait crop **4:5** (e.g. 1200×1500), tight to the
  subject's head/shoulders, framed `object-top`.
- **Filename:** exactly the slug below — `<slug>.webp`.
- **Direction:** premium editorial photography of a person performing real
  institutional work (signing, reviewing, presiding) — not a smiling office
  cliché, not a stock-photo feel.

## Required files (featured cards)

| File | Role | Institution | Region | For balance |
|------|------|-------------|--------|-------------|
| `minister-health.webp` | Minister of Health | Government | Africa | woman |
| `university-registrar.webp` | University Registrar | University | Europe | man |
| `corporate-secretary.webp` | Corporate Secretary | Enterprise | Asia | woman |
| `hospital-director.webp` | Hospital Director | Healthcare | Middle East | man |

| `national-archivist.webp` | National Archivist | Archives | Africa | man |

## Representation

Span **Africa, Europe, Asia, the Middle East and the Americas**, with **equal
representation of men and women**. The roles below the featured cards
("Across every institution") are text-only and need no photography.

> To rotate which roles are featured (or add more), edit `FEATURED` in
> `src/pages/Landing.tsx`. Each entry's `slug` maps to `<slug>.webp` here.
